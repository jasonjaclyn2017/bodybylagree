Privacy Policy

Body By Lagree Society · Last updated: August 30, 2026

Body By Lagree Society ("we", "us") operates the website bodybylagree.studio and a Lagree fitness studio in San Antonio, Texas. This policy describes what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide. When you book a class, purchase a membership, or submit a form, we collect details such as your name, email address, and phone number. Class booking and payments are processed by our studio-management provider, Kenko (gokenko.com); payment card details are handled by Kenko and its payment processors and are never stored by us.
  • Usage information. We use analytics tools — Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and the Meta Pixel — to understand how visitors use our site (pages visited, device type, approximate location, and interactions). These tools may use cookies or similar technologies.

How we use information

  • To provide and manage class bookings, memberships, and customer support.
  • To send service communications about your bookings or account.
  • To send marketing communications you have opted into (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising, including showing you relevant ads on platforms such as Facebook and Instagram.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the studio (booking and payments via Kenko, analytics and advertising via Google, Microsoft, and Meta), or when required by law.

Your choices

  • You can opt out of marketing emails or texts at any time via the unsubscribe link or by replying STOP.
  • You can control cookies through your browser settings, and opt out of Meta ad targeting in your Facebook ad preferences.
  • To access, correct, or delete your personal information, contact us at the email below.

Data retention & security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above and protect it using industry-standard safeguards. Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
